02//About us

A Klaipeda ship repair company, working worldwide

UAB "Litprofit" was established in 2010. More than a decade in the refrigeration equipment market, a long list of completed projects, and business partners who have stayed.

01//Capability

What we specialise in

  1. 01Design and selection of industrial refrigeration equipment.
  2. 02Modernisation and repair of refrigeration systems for fishing boats and shore installations.
  3. 03Installation and supply of refrigeration equipment.

02//The people

Reliable, qualified, time-tested

We have a team of reliable, qualified and time-tested professionals, and we provide a warranty on all works and equipment.

We are committed to continuous improvement and pay close attention to developments in the market. We take each customer's wishes into account, and aim to offer the solution that is most sensible on cost, on time, and on the equipment supplied.
