What the PRS approval covers

Certificate TM/1703/842502/25 approves UAB „Litprofit“ to carry out repairs of:

  • Main and auxiliary engines — two-stroke and four-stroke
  • Deck machinery and mechanisms
  • Fuel equipment of main and auxiliary engines, compressors and pumps
  • Turbochargers
  • Reduction gears
  • Alignment of diesel engines
  • Refrigeration equipment and systems

It also approves the design of refrigeration equipment and systems — not only their repair.

What these cover

RINA is an Italian classification society; PRS is the Polish Register of Shipping. Certification by a class society is what lets a shipowner accept our work and documentation without commissioning a separate inspection to verify it.

Insurance

The company's civil liability is insured with Compensa Vienna Insurance Group, ADB for EUR 250,000, under insurance policy no. 230 0008143 / 2020.

Warranty

We provide a warranty on all works and equipment. All equipment and spare parts manufactured and supplied by the company comply with quality and international standards.

24/7 service

We are ready to provide prompt and competent assistance — tell us the vessel, the equipment and the port, and we will come back with a plan.
