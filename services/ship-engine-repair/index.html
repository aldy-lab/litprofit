Services 02

Refrigeration pump and valve repair

Keeping refrigeration pumps and valves inside their operating range — for marine and shore systems, and for the pipework the crew depends on.

Refrigerant pump repair

We repair and select refrigerant pumps for marine and shore systems. Work runs from diagnostics through to full restoration of working condition, including sourcing equivalent spare parts and components where they are needed.

Pump manufacturers we work with:

  • HERMETIC
  • EDUR
  • Teikoku
  • Cornell
  • Warrender
  • Nikkiso
  • Hydrodyne
  • F.N. Engineering

Valve repair and selection

We repair and select shut-off and regulating valves for refrigeration systems, matching them to the existing pipework and to the requirements of the project.

Valve manufacturers we work with:

  • Danfoss
  • Castel
  • Parker
  • Emerson
  • Sporlan
  • Carly
  • Henry Technologies
  • Alco Controls

Pipework connection and installation

We carry out pipework connection and equipment installation for projects of any size, allowing for what marine and shore systems each require.

How we work

We use quality tooling and vetted sources of spare parts, and every repair is carried out to meet the applicable safety standards. After installation we pressure-test and evacuate the whole system before it is put into service. All work and equipment carries our warranty.
