About us
Our straggly and ambitious enterprise is guided by today's needs. The company has a professional team of specialists who are ready to advise and fulfill all your orders
Contact us
We consult
Our consultations are guided by our experience, future trends, your wishes and possibilities. We devote exceptional attention and time to each customer.
We organise

„LITPROFIT”, UAB was established in 2010. We have been successfully operating in the refrigeration equipment market for more than ten years. During this time, we have implemented many projects, acquired solid and reliable business partners.
„LITPROFIT”, UAB specialises in:
·         Design and selection of industrial refrigeration equipment;
·         Modernisation and repair of refrigeration systems for fishing boats and shore installations (GRASSO/KUHLAUTOMAT, HOWDEN, MYCOM, SABROE, STAL, AERZEN, YORK DYKIN, HITACHI etc.);
·         Installation and supply of refrigeration equipment;
·         Overhaul of main and auxiliary engines (MAN, Wartsila, Yanmar, Hyundai Himsen, MAK, Caterpillar, Deutz, Daihatsu, and other manufacturers);
· Positioning of ships in the port of Klaipėda (Lithuania) for repair works.
„LITPROFIT“, UAB has been successfully cooperating with various well-known companies for many years.
We have a team of reliable, qualified and time-tested professionals. We provide a warranty on all works and equipment.
We are committed to continuous improvement and are greatly interested in market innovations, we take into account the wishes and suggestions of each customer, and we strive to offer our customers the most optimal solution in terms of cost and time, as well as the equipment supplied.
„LITPROFIT” has been awarded the RINA certificate.
The civil liability of Litprofit , UAB is insured with Compensa Vienna Insurance Group, ADB for the amount of EUR 250,000,00. Insurance policy No 230 0008143 / 2020.

Services /
Ship equipment and engine repair
Ship equipment and engine repair
Our ship equipment and engine repair service aims to ensure the optimal and safe operation of your vessel or other technical equipment. We offer a wide range of services to meet your needs:
1. Engine repair:
4-stroke diesel engine repair: Our team is an expert in 4-stroke diesel engines. We carry out comprehensive diagnostic procedures and upgrades to ensure engine efficiency and durability.
2-stroke diesel engine repair: If your ship or other equipment uses 2-stroke diesel engines, we have the experience and expertise to carry out any necessary repairs.
2. Machinery maintenance:
Maintenance of other machinery in the engine room: We specialise in the maintenance of ship engines, as well as other machinery in the engine room. This may include maintenance and repair of speeders, shafts, and other machinery.
3. Maintenance and repair of deck equipment:
Maintenance of deck equipment: Deck equipment is an essential part of the ship, so we offer regular maintenance to ensure its proper operation. This includes the maintenance of deck systems, steps, life-saving appliances and other deck equipment.
Deck equipment repair : If your deck equipment has been damaged or broken, we will carry out the necessary repairs to get it back to perfect working condition.
We use only high-quality tools and sources of spare parts to ensure the efficiency and durability of our work. We also work hard to guarantee that all repairs meet the highest safety standards.
If you have any questions or would like to receive more information about our services, please do not hesitate to contact us. We are here to help you maintain your vessel or technical equipment in excellent condition!

Refrigeration systems and equipment
Refrigeration systems and equipment
LITPROFIT carries out the following work:
Diagnostics and repair of compressors for refrigeration equipment of different complexity;
Diagnostics and repair of commercial and marine refrigeration equipment;
Consultancy on the selection, installation and maintenance of refrigeration equipment;
Development of automatic control systems for compressors and refrigeration equipment;
Preparation of design documents and working drawings for ship refrigeration equipment in accordance with the requirements of the classification societies, including their approval;
Installation of refrigeration equipment and refrigerant piping;
Start-up and adjustment of refrigeration equipment and delivery to the client.
We provide both one-off and regular (contracted) service work.
All equipment and spare parts manufactured and supplied by our company comply with quality and international standards.
We provide compressor maintenance services of the following manufacturers:
SABROE (Denmark)
BITZER (Germany)
HOWDEN (Scotland)
KUHLAUTOMAT (Germany)
STAL (Sweden)
HALLSCREW (England)
GRASSO (Netherlands)

Supply of spare parts
